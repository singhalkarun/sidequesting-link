Delete your SideQuesting account

Applies to the SideQuesting app by Ambition Forge Private Limited

In the app (instant)

Profile → settings (gear) → Delete account → type DELETE to confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Without the app

Email hello@atominfra.com with the subject "Delete my SideQuesting account" from the Google account email you signed up with. We verify it's you and delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Everything: your profile, handle, photos, posts, comments, likes, points, streaks, goal history, phone number, and push tokens. This can't be undone. Crash and analytics records are de-identified. We don't retain your content after deletion.
