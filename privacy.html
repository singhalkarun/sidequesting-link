Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

SideQuesting ("the app") is owned and operated by Ambition Forge Private Limited ("we"). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what happens to it.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address and nameWhen you sign in with GoogleCreating and securing your account
Handle and profile details (photo, display name)When you set themYour public profile
Phone number (optional)If you add oneNever shown publicly; used so quest partners you choose can be matched to you
PhotosWhen you complete a quest with photo proofYour proof and, if you share it, your feed post
Approximate location (~100 m) and area nameOnly for location-based quests, only when you complete oneAttached to that proof; optional and user-initiated
Usage analytics (device identifiers, screens used, actions taken)While you use the appUnderstanding what works so we can improve the app
Crash logs and diagnosticsIf the app crashes or errorsFixing bugs
Push notification tokenIf you allow notificationsDelivering notifications (partner requests, verifications, weekly quests)

Who processes it

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone except the services that run the app on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage (your account and content live here)
  • Google — sign-in, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push delivery
  • Expo — app updates and push notification routing
  • PostHog — usage analytics
  • Sentry — crash reporting

What other users see

Your profile (handle, name, photo, points, public quest completions) is visible to other users. Posts you share to the feed are visible to users who aren't blocked. Completions you mark private are visible only to you. Your email and phone number are never shown to anyone.

Security and retention

All data travels over HTTPS. We keep your data until you delete your account; deletion permanently removes your profile, posts, photos, points, streaks, goal history, and phone number.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → settings → Delete account. Or request deletion without the app at sidequesting.club/delete-account.

Children

SideQuesting is not directed at children, and you must be at least 16 to use it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially we'll note it here with a new date. Questions: hello@atominfra.com.
