Who processes it

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone except the services that run the app on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage (your account and content live here)
  • Google — sign-in, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push delivery
  • Expo — app updates and push notification routing
  • PostHog — usage analytics
  • Sentry — crash reporting

What other users see

Your profile (handle, name, photo, points, public quest completions) is visible to other users. Posts you share to the feed are visible to users who aren't blocked. Completions you mark private are visible only to you. Your email and phone number are never shown to anyone.

Steps and Apple Health / Health Connect

If you add a steps goal, SideQuesting can — with your permission — read your step count from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android). Your step count is used for exactly one thing: showing your progress on that goal and marking it done when you reach your target. Health data stays on your phone. We never upload it, never store it on our servers, never share it with anyone, and never use it for advertising or analytics. When your goal is marked done, only the fact that the goal was completed — never the step count — is saved to your account, exactly as if you had tapped it yourself. You can turn this off any time in SideQuesting’s Settings (“Read steps from my phone”), in iOS Settings → Privacy → Health, or in Health Connect. Turning it off deletes nothing and breaks nothing — the goal simply goes back to being ticked by hand.

Security and retention

All data travels over HTTPS. We keep your data until you delete your account; deletion permanently removes your profile, posts, photos, points, streaks, goal history, and phone number.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → settings → Delete account. Or request deletion without the app at sidequesting.club/delete-account.

Children

SideQuesting is not directed at children, and you must be at least 16 to use it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially we'll note it here with a new date. Questions: support@sidequesting.club.
